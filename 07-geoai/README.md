# GeoAI for Geospatial & Environmental Applications

This section will contain reproducible GeoAI work connecting geospatial data science with climate, land and environmental applications.

## Application themes
- Land use/land cover classification and change detection.
- Land degradation and ecosystem-condition monitoring.
- Flood susceptibility and exposure analysis.
- Habitat and restoration suitability modelling.
- Climate vulnerability and spatial risk analysis.
- Object detection or feature extraction from aerial and satellite imagery.

These are areas of interest and potential application, not a claim that every model has already been implemented.

## Two complementary strands
1. **Technical implementation:** data preparation, feature engineering, model development, training, testing, validation, spatial interpretation and communication of results.
2. **Proposal and project design:** defining the problem, assessing data readiness, selecting justified methods, planning validation, estimating compute/data needs, defining deliverables and identifying model risks.

## Minimum standard for each GeoAI case study
- Decision problem and intended users.
- Study area and spatial/temporal scale.
- Data sources, licence, dates and spatial resolution.
- Label/reference data and sampling strategy.
- Train/validation/test split, including spatial separation where appropriate.
- Baseline and candidate model(s), features and parameters.
- Appropriate evaluation metrics and uncertainty.
- Error analysis, bias and limitations.
- Reproducible code, environment/dependencies and processing instructions.
- Maps, tables and interpretation of operational relevance.

## Responsible use
Avoid data leakage, random-only validation where spatial dependence makes it misleading, unsupported accuracy claims and opaque model outputs. Do not use sensitive or restricted data without permission. Explain where expert review is needed and distinguish model predictions from observed facts.
