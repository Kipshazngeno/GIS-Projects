# Climate, Land Degradation & Environmental Applications

This section is intended for applied geospatial work supporting climate resilience, environmental planning and natural-resource management.

## Relevant themes
- Climate hazard, exposure, vulnerability and risk mapping
- Land degradation assessment and land degradation neutrality (LDN) support
- Land use/land cover mapping and change analysis
- Ecosystem condition, restoration and conservation mapping
- Environmental indicators and spatial decision support
- Integration of satellite, field, climate, population and sector datasets

## Case-study standard
For each project, state the decision problem, study area, indicators, datasets and dates, analytical method, validation approach, limitations, outputs and how the results can inform planning. Distinguish completed analysis from proposed methodology.
