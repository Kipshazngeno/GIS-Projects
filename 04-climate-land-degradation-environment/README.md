# Climate, Land Degradation & Environmental Applications

This section curates applied geospatial work supporting climate resilience, environmental planning, ecosystem management and sustainable land use.

## Thematic focus
- Climate change adaptation and resilience.
- Climate hazard, exposure, vulnerability and risk assessment.
- Land degradation assessment, restoration and land degradation neutrality support.
- Land use/land cover mapping and change analysis.
- Ecosystem restoration, wetland and river-corridor monitoring.
- Disaster risk management and climate-related health risk mapping.
- Environmental indicators and spatial decision support.
- GeoAI-enabled environmental analysis.

## Recommended technical evidence
For each project, document the decision problem, geographic scope, datasets and dates, indicators, analytical methods, stakeholder or technical validation, outputs, limitations and intended use. For climate risk, distinguish hazard, exposure, vulnerability and risk. For land degradation, document indicator definitions, baseline/reference periods and data lineage. For LULC, report the classification scheme, training/reference data, validation design and accuracy metrics where available.

## Publication standard
Do not imply a model, map or assessment has been completed where it is only proposed. Do not publish confidential project data. Clearly distinguish your own contribution from team or institutional outputs.
