# Digital Photogrammetry & UAV Mapping

This section covers traditional aerial photogrammetry and UAV/drone image processing.

## Existing technical manuals
- Digital Photogrammetry and Drone Image Processing Manual (2026)
- Drone Imagery Processing in ArcGIS Pro (June 2025)

## Documented workflow themes
- Photogrammetric project and camera setup
- Image orientation: inner, relative and absolute
- Fiducial marks, tie points and pass points
- Ground control and aerial triangulation
- Triangulation reports and RMSE review
- Drone flight planning and image acquisition
- ArcGIS Pro Ortho Mapping and Pix4D workflows
- DSM/DTM, contours, orthophotos/orthomosaics, point clouds and 3D products

The 2025 ArcGIS Pro exercise uses imagery acquired over the RCMRD complex in Nairobi with a DJI Phantom 4 RTK. The exercise describes block adjustment without GCPs, then DSM and orthomosaic generation. Do not represent this exercise as an independently surveyed accuracy assessment.

## Recommended additions
Add permitted sample imagery, flight parameters, GSD, overlap, camera details, coordinate reference systems, GCP/checkpoint design, processing reports, horizontal/vertical accuracy results and annotated final outputs. Clearly label any tutorial or training dataset.
