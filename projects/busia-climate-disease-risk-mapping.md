# Project Record: Busia Climate and Disease Risk Mapping

**Theme:** Climate-sensitive health risk, flood hazards and geospatial decision support  
**Geography:** Busia County, Kenya  
**Portfolio status:** Project experience record; detailed personal role, methods and delivered outputs to be verified.

## Context
The project concerns the use of geospatial evidence to understand relationships between climate and environmental conditions, flood hazards, population exposure, disease patterns and health-service considerations in selected locations.

## Technical areas to document
- Data inventory covering climate, environmental, epidemiological, population and health-service information.
- Flood hazard mapping and spatial analysis.
- Disease pattern analysis using available epidemiological data.
- Population and health-service exposure assessment.
- Vulnerability considerations and identification of higher-risk populations.
- Integration of satellite, drone or AI-enabled methods where technically justified.
- Validation with relevant county, health and community stakeholders.
- Decision-support maps and concise products for planning and preparedness.
