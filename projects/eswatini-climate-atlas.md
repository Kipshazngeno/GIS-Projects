# Project Record: Eswatini Climate Atlas

**Theme:** Climate information, climate-risk assessment and decision support  
**Geography:** Eswatini  
**Portfolio status:** Project experience record; detailed personal role and completed outputs to be verified against approved project records.

## Context
The project concerns bringing together climate information from multiple sources, including meteorological station records and other relevant climate datasets, to support an accessible and scientifically traceable climate atlas. Intended applications include climate reporting, adaptation planning, disaster risk management, climate services and investment prioritisation.

## Technical areas to document
- Climate data inventory, access, quality assessment and management.
- Harmonisation and spatial database development.
- Climate baseline and seasonal analysis.
- Climate hazard and exposure mapping.
- Vulnerability and risk assessment.
- Stakeholder engagement, technical validation and governance.
- Atlas/platform requirements, map production and knowledge transfer.
- Data lineage, metadata, reproducibility and reporting traceability.
