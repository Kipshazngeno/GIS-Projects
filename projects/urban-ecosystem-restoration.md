# Project Record: Urban Ecosystem Restoration

**Theme:** Urban natural ecosystem assessment and restoration planning  
**Geography:** Nairobi, Kenya (study scope to be confirmed for the published case study)  
**Portfolio status:** Research/project concept and technical contribution record; confirm whether each component is proposed, ongoing or completed.

## Problem focus
Urban growth can place pressure on wetlands, river corridors and connected green spaces. Geospatial analysis can help identify land-cover change, encroachment, fragmentation and opportunities for ecosystem restoration and green infrastructure.

## Technical areas to document
- Multi-temporal satellite imagery and LULC mapping.
- Wetland extent and encroachment analysis.
- River-corridor condition and riparian land-cover assessment.
- Green-space connectivity and fragmentation.
- Change detection and spatial metrics.
- Urban heat, surface-water or other environmental indicators where data and methods support them.
- Restoration opportunity mapping and prioritisation.
- Map-based communication for planning and decision support.

## Proposal and project-design contribution
Document the role in framing the research/problem statement, selecting geospatial methods and indicators, developing the workplan and deliverables, and preparing technical or proposal material.

## Evidence to add
Study-area boundary and rationale; imagery sources, dates and preprocessing; classification scheme and validation evidence; change maps and statistics; restoration criteria and prioritisation method; limitations, recommendations and publication permissions.

## Reporting integrity
Clearly distinguish a proposed methodology, a research analysis and an implemented restoration intervention. Do not claim ecological recovery or restoration impact from land-cover maps alone.
