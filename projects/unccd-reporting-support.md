# Project Record: UNCCD Reporting Support

**Theme:** Land degradation information, geospatial evidence and reporting capacity  
**Geography:** Regional support context; country-specific activities to be confirmed

## Context
Geospatial information and consistent land indicators can support countries in assessing land degradation, tracking change and preparing evidence for reporting under the United Nations Convention to Combat Desertification (UNCCD).

## Technical areas to document
- Geospatial data and indicator preparation.
- Land use/land cover and land-cover change analysis.
- Land productivity and relevant land degradation indicators.
- Soil organic carbon information where appropriate data are available.
- Data quality, metadata and indicator traceability.
- Use of relevant reporting platforms and workflows, where applicable.
- Technical training, user support and capacity development.
- Interpretation of results and preparation of reporting evidence.
